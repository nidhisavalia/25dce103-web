Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. What is StudentHub?

StudentHub is a website that helps students access academic information and services.

2. How do I register?

Go to the Register page, fill in your details, and click the Register button.

3. How do I log in?

Enter your email and password on the Login page and click Login.

4. Can I update my profile?

Yes, you can update your profile from the Profile page.

5. How can I contact StudentHub?

You can use the Contact Us page or email us at studenthub@gmail.com.
